FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical project take?
▼
Most projects fall between 3 and 8 weeks depending on scope. A simple marketing site or MVP takes 2–3 weeks. A full SaaS platform with custom auth, dashboards, and integrations typically runs 6–10 weeks. We give you a fixed timeline in the proposal so there are no surprises.
Do I own the code after the project?
▼
Yes, 100%. At handoff you receive the complete source code, repository access, and all architecture documentation. There are no ongoing license fees or lock-in. You can host it anywhere and hire any developer to maintain it.
What if I only have an idea, not a full spec?
▼
That's completely fine — most clients come to us with an idea rather than a detailed spec. Our discovery process helps you define the right features, architecture, and scope. We'll create the technical spec together before development begins.
Can you work with our existing codebase?
▼
Yes. We regularly take over, refactor, or extend existing codebases. We'll audit what you have, flag technical debt, and propose the most efficient path forward — whether that's building on top of it or recommending a strategic rebuild.
Do you offer maintenance after the 30-day support period?
▼
Yes, we offer optional monthly retainer plans for ongoing maintenance, feature additions, and technical support. Many clients stay on a retainer after launch. Retainer pricing is discussed separately based on your expected monthly needs.
How are projects priced?
▼
We work on fixed-price project contracts — no hourly surprises. After the discovery call, we send a detailed proposal with a locked scope, price, and timeline. Payment is milestone-based (typically 40% upfront, 30% at mid-point, 30% at delivery). We don't do open-ended hourly billing.
